The viral "How Wolves Change Rivers" video is getting a fresh round of scientific pushback

The 2014 short (40M+ views) credited Yellowstone's reintroduced wolves with regrowing forests and even shifting rivers. Long-term Utah State and Colorado State studies now say that "trophic cascade" was overstated — willow and river recovery is modest and patchy, and tied as much to climate, hydrology, beaver and bison as to wolves.
